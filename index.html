Hi, I'm Sarah

I'm a data engineer and a curious AI enthusiast based in Dhahran. For about seven years I've been turning messy source data into things people can trust: warehouses, ETL pipelines, reconciliations and dashboards.

My favourite part is the bit most people skip: matching source to target, writing automated quality checks, and tracing every odd number back to its root cause. I sharpened that on financial-crime and forensic work at Deloitte, and before that by building an accounts-receivable warehouse from scratch, including a full database migration and automated monthly reporting for every business unit.

These days I build cloud lakehouses end to end and keep one eye on AI: my MSc thesis was a sentiment-based recommender system, and I'm certified in managing AI projects. I also enjoy explaining a tricky design in plain words, in Arabic or English.
