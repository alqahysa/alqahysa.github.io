Security: how your token is handled
  • Your token is kept only in sessionStorage for this browser tab. It is never written to the code, to projects.json or to the repository, and it is erased when you close the tab or press “Forget token”.
  • Use a fine-grained personal access token limited to the single repository alqahysa.github.io with Contents: Read and write only, and a short expiry (for example 7–30 days).
  • Only use this page on your own device. Never commit a token, paste it into projects.json, or share a screenshot that shows it.
